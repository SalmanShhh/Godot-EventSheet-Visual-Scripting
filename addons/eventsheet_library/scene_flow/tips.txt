Hold the run button while you jump to clear the widest gaps.
Standing still for a moment refills the last bar of your shield.
Most locked doors have a second way in somewhere above them.
You can sell anything you are carrying back at the harbour.
Enemies that glow have already noticed you.
Reading a signpost marks the place it names on your map.
Falling never costs more than the height you fell from.
The map remembers every room you have walked into, even the empty ones.
